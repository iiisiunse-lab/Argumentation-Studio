Argumentation Studio

An interactive companion to six lines of research by Dr. Maximiliano Celmo David Budán. Co-author researchers — Paola D. Budán, Melisa G. Escañuela Gonzalez, Diego C. Martínez, María Vanina Martinez, Gerardo I. Simari and Guillermo R. Simari. Research fellows — Irene M. Coronel and Carlos Habiñak.

Six formalisms, one thread: reasoning with arguments that support and attack each other, enriched with labels, similarity, interpretations, social endorsement, hashtags and graded strength — and made to talk to one another. Each tab below is a self-contained, faithful implementation of the corresponding paper, with all worked examples ready to run.

L-BAF

Labeled strength

Propagate multi-feature label algebras through support and attack; gradual status, value-based extensions and pruning.

S-BAF

Communities

Detect discursive communities by argument similarity; measure cohesion and controversy, and assimilate coalitions.

P. D. Budán, M. G. Escañuela Gonzalez, M. C. D. Budán, M. V. Martinez, G. R. Simari. “Similarity notions in bipolar abstract argumentation”. Argument & Computation 11(1-2):103–149, 2020. doi:10.3233/AAC-190479 Σ
IAF

Interpretations
